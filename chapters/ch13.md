## Where to find language resources
- worldcat (worldcat.org)
- google scholar (scholar.google.com)
- glottolog (glottolog.org)
- OLAC (language-archives.org) ** frequently unavailable these days
- Regional archives:
	- Paradisec (Australasia)
	- AILLA (Latin America)
	- CLA (Berkeley linguists + languages of California)
- Other archives
	- ELAR (worldwide)
	- Pangloss/Lacito

Online text collections:
There's an increasing number of community-oriented text collections and things like that online. They can be quite fragile in terms of links though
